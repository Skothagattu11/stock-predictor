Platform overview

What this tool is, and what it is not

This platform is a live equity research and simulation environment. It streams real-time price data, computes technical indicators, generates AI-powered research reports, and lets you simulate trades — all in one browser tab. It is designed to help you form a view faster and stress-test ideas before committing real capital.

Not financial advice. Every signal, report, and recommendation on this platform is an analytical prompt, not a directive. Apply your own judgement before acting on behalf of clients.

The platform has five interconnected tools:

ToolPurpose
Signal DashboardLive chart, technical indicators, candle patterns, BUY / SELL / WAIT signal
AI Equity AnalystInstitutional-style research report for any US ticker — BUY / HOLD / SELL with confidence score
Wealth ManagerClient and portfolio tracker with live P&L, shareable read-only links
Paper TradingSimulated trade book at live prices — test strategies with zero risk
Pine Script BridgeAuto-execute or queue trades from TradingView Pine Script alerts via webhook

Signal Dashboard

Live candlestick chart · indicators · pattern detection · signals

Searching for a ticker

Use the search box in the chart header. Start typing a company name or ticker symbol — results appear as you type. Click a result to load the live chart.

The live chart

The chart streams real-time candles over WebSocket when markets are open. Use the controls in the header to change:

ControlOptions
Candle size1m · 5m · 15m · 30m · 1h · 1 day · 1 week
History range1D · 5D · 1M · 3M · 6M · YTD · 1Y · 5Y · Max
Chart styleCandles · Line
SimulateSwitches to a synthetic data feed — useful for demos when markets are closed

Overlaid on the chart are: EMA 9 (fast, blue), EMA 20 (mid, amber), EMA 50 (slow, purple), Bollinger Bands (dashed), VWAP (session), Supertrend, and volume bars.

The signal box

The signal box on the right shows the current market signal for the loaded ticker:

BUY / LONG ≥ 64 SELL / SHORT ≤ 36 WAIT 37–63

The score (0–100) is built from a weighted blend of: VWAP position, Supertrend direction, Opening Range Breakout, EMA alignment, RSI, MACD histogram, volume pulse, and detected candle patterns.

Below the signal is the Entry / Exit plan — machine-calculated buy trigger, stop loss, and sell/trim target levels based on current structure.

Indicator snapshot

Scrolling down you'll find the live indicator readout: RSI 14, EMA stack, volume pulse, MACD histogram, ATR 14, VWAP and its session position, Supertrend direction, opening range, and Bollinger Band levels.

Your position tracker

Enter any two of — average price per share, number of shares, or total order value — and the tool computes your unrealized P&L and gives you a personalized call (HOLD · TAKE PROFIT · SELL / TRIM · CUT LOSS) based on your cost basis relative to the current signal and price structure.

AI assistant

The floating chat widget (bottom right, labelled Ask the assistant) lets you ask free-text questions about the current chart, your position, or the market. Enable the Web toggle to let the assistant search the internet for context.

Market news

The collapsible News section pulls the latest headlines for the loaded ticker from Finnhub and Yahoo Finance. Articles open in a new tab.

AI Equity Analyst

BUY / HOLD / SELL research reports with confidence scoring

The Analyst page generates an institutional-style equity research report in under 15 seconds. Data is pulled from live market feeds; the AI writes the qualitative analysis on top. Numbers are never invented by the model.

Running a report

  1. Navigate to /analyst.html or click the Analyst pill in the top navigation.
  2. Type a US ticker in the search box. Results filter as you type — click to select, or type the symbol exactly (e.g. NVDA).
  3. Choose depth: Standard for a quick report, Deep for peer benchmarking.
  4. Click Analyse or press Enter. The report appears within ~10–15 seconds.

Side-by-side comparison

Type two or more tickers separated by commas to run a comparison:

AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL

The tool runs a report for each ticker in parallel, then renders a ranked comparison table with an AI-generated rationale for the winner.

Forcing a fresh report

Reports are cached for 30 minutes. After an earnings release or major event, force a fresh run by appending ?fresh=1:

/analyst.html?ticker=AAPL&fresh=1

Verdict and confidence score

The verdict (BUY / HOLD / SELL) at the top comes from AI analysis of the narrative. The confidence score (0–100%) is calculated from seven independent technical and fundamental signals:

vs SMA 200Price above the 200-day moving average

Circuit breaker: if the confidence score falls below 50%, the verdict is automatically overridden to HOLD — regardless of the AI's narrative. This prevents a bullish recommendation from going out when the underlying signals are mixed.

Green ≥ 80% confidence · Amber 50–79% · Red < 50%

Placing a paper trade from a report

Every report banner has a Place Paper Trade → button. It takes you to the signal dashboard pre-loaded with that ticker, ready to open a simulated position.

Wealth Manager

Client and portfolio tracker with live P&L and shareable links

Signing in

  1. Go to /login.html and enter your credentials (or register a new account).
  2. On success you are redirected to /manager.html. Your session stays active for the browser session; signing out clears it.

Creating clients and portfolios

  1. Click + Add Client and enter the client's name. The client is saved immediately.
  2. Click a client row to expand it, then click + New Portfolio (e.g. "Growth", "Income", "Retirement").
  3. Inside a portfolio, click + Add Position and fill in the ticker, number of shares, and entry price.

Every position shows live price, market value, unrealized P&L, and P&L %. Rows update automatically. The portfolio footer shows the total market value and aggregate P&L.

Running an analyst report from a position

Each position row has an Analyst button. Clicking it opens a pre-filled analyst report for that ticker in a new tab.

Sharing a portfolio

  1. Open a portfolio and click Share.
  2. A unique link is generated (e.g. /share.html?token=abc123…).
  3. Send the link to the client. The page shows positions, live prices, and P&L — no login required.
  4. You can revoke the link or set an expiry date from the same Share panel.
Privacy: the share token is a long random UUID. Only people with the exact link can see the portfolio. The Wealth Manager API is fully isolated per account — you cannot see another manager's clients.

Paper Trading

Simulated trades at live prices — zero risk, real price action

The Paper Trading panel is on the main signal dashboard (/). It gives you a virtual $10,000 account that trades at actual live prices, so you can test a strategy or a signal without committing real money.

Account settings

SettingWhat it does
Auto-take every pickWhen checked, the system automatically opens a paper position whenever the dashboard generates a BUY signal for the current ticker
Budget $Dollar amount to allocate per trade
Target $Profit target per trade (triggers a nudge to close)
RiskCautious / Balanced / Aggressive — controls the stop-loss tightness

Opening a position manually

  1. Load a ticker on the signal dashboard.
  2. In the Paper Trading panel, confirm the budget and risk settings.
  3. The Goal Planner section (just above Paper Trading) can help you find tickers where your profit target is realistic — click any result's Paper Trade button to pre-load it.
  4. Once auto-mode is on, or via the analyst report's Place Paper Trade button, the order opens at the current live price.

Open positions

Each open position shows: symbol, shares, entry price, live price, and unrealized P&L in real time. Two action buttons are available:

  • Analyst — opens an AI research report for the position's ticker in a new tab
  • Close — closes the position at the current live price and records the trade in history

Trade history

Closed trades appear below the open positions with entry price, exit price, shares, and realized P&L. Win rate and aggregate realized P&L are shown in the account summary bar.

Resetting the account

Click Reset account to wipe all positions and history and return to the starting $10,000 balance. This is permanent — there is no undo.

Pine Script / TradingView Bridge

Route TradingView alerts into the paper book — automatically or with approval

The Pine Script Bridge lets you connect a TradingView strategy to the paper trading book. When your Pine Script fires an alert, it sends a webhook to this platform. Depending on your settings, the trade either executes immediately or is queued for your approval.

Step 1 — Configure the bridge settings

Expand the Pine Script / TradingView Signals section in the Paper Trading panel on the dashboard:

SettingDescription
Enable auto-trading from Pine alerts Master on/off switch. Must be checked for any signals to be processed.
Auto mode Signals execute immediately as paper trades the moment the alert fires.
Approval mode Signals are queued for your review. A "Pending Pine Signals" panel appears — you approve or reject each one manually.
When already holding the ticker Skip — ignore the duplicate signal (default for most strategies)
Stack — open a second independent position
Scale — add to the existing position at the new price
On EXIT / SELL signal Close all — close every open position for that ticker
Close most recent — close only the newest position
Ignore — do not close automatically (manual only)
Trade budget $ Dollar amount allocated per Pine-triggered trade. Separate from the manual paper trading budget.

Step 2 — Set up the TradingView webhook

In TradingView, open your Pine Script strategy or study → Alert dialog → set Webhook URL to:

http://<your-server>/api/tv/webhook

Set the Message to a JSON payload with this format:

{
  "symbol":   "{{ticker}}",
  "action":   "buy",
  "price":    {{close}},
  "strategy": "My Strategy Name"
}
Accepted values for action:
buy or long — opens a long paper position
sell, short, or exit — triggers the EXIT behaviour you configured above

Step 3 — Approve or auto-execute

Once a signal arrives:

  • Auto mode — the paper trade appears in open positions immediately. Target is set to +6% above entry, stop to −3% below entry.
  • Approval mode — the signal appears in the Pending Pine Signals panel (yellow count badge). Click Approve to execute or Reject to discard. The panel refreshes every 15 seconds.
Best practice for live testing: start with Approval mode so you can review each signal before it executes. Once you are confident the strategy is firing correctly, switch to Auto for hands-off operation.

What happens when a signal fires (Auto mode)

Signal typeBehaviour
BUY — no existing position Opens a paper long at the signal price. Budget = Pine trade budget. Target = price × 1.06. Stop = price × 0.97.
BUY — existing position, onDuplicate = skip Signal is silently discarded. No second position opened.
BUY — existing position, onDuplicate = stack A second independent position is opened for the same ticker.
SELL / EXIT — onExit = closeAll Every open paper position for that ticker is closed at current price.
SELL / EXIT — onExit = closeLatest Only the most recently opened position for that ticker is closed.
SELL / EXIT — onExit = ignore No automatic close. You must close positions manually.

Reading signals

What each output means and how to use it

Market signal (the stock)

SignalScoreMeaning
BUY / LONG ≥ 64 Multiple indicators align bullishly. Favourable structure for a long entry — but confirm at the entry / exit plan levels before acting.
SELL / SHORT ≤ 36 Bearish confluence. Favours reducing or exiting a long, or entering short. Verify against your own thesis before acting.
WAIT 37–63 No clean edge. Mixed or conflicting signals — the model recommends staying out until structure improves.

Personalized position call

CallMeaning
HOLDTrend still supports the position — no action needed.
HOLD / ADDBullish signal while you are underwater — hold, only add with a plan.
TAKE PROFITPrice reached your target zone — consider locking gains.
SELL / TRIMBearish signal while you are in profit — protect gains.
REDUCE / TIGHTEN STOPBearish while you are down — cut exposure or tighten your stop.
CUT LOSSPrice hit your stop zone — close the position.

Analyst report verdicts

VerdictConfidenceInterpretation
BUY ≥ 80% Strong fundamental and technical alignment. AI analysis and quantitative signals agree. Worth a closer look for a long position.
HOLD 50–79% Mixed signals. The stock may be fairly valued or in a neutral technical zone. Watch rather than act.
SELL Any Bearish fundamental or technical factors outweigh positives. Consider reducing or exiting exposure.
Signal latency: WebSocket data streams in real time during market hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET). Outside market hours the chart uses the most recent session's closing candles and indicators are computed from that data. The signal box will reflect the after-hours status.

Indicator reference

IndicatorWhat to look for
RSI 14> 70 = overbought (potential reversal); < 30 = oversold (potential bounce); 40–60 = neutral.
MACD histogramPositive and growing = bullish momentum. Negative = bearish. Watch for crossovers.
EMA 9 / 20 / 50Price above all three EMAs in ascending order = bullish trend. Price below = bearish.
VWAPPrice above VWAP intraday = institutional buying. Below = selling pressure.
SupertrendGreen (below price) = uptrend. Red (above price) = downtrend.
Bollinger BandsPrice near upper band = extended / possible mean-reversion. Near lower = oversold zone.
ATR 14Measures volatility. Use to size positions and set stop distances.
Volume pulseHigh = conviction behind the move. Low volume on a breakout = weak signal.

Admin / system health

Visit /admin.html to check live connector status, cache hit rate, average report time, and request/error history. This is useful before a client meeting to verify all data feeds are healthy.

FMP (Financial Modeling Prep) has a free-tier limit of 250 calls per day, supporting approximately 40 full analyst reports. The admin page shows current quota usage.
